# NTU EEE MSc ICME Study Resources

My personal study resources for the **Master of Science in Integrated Circuits & Microelectronics (ICME)** at the **School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE), Nanyang Technological University, Singapore**.

I prepare and organize these materials from my coursework, revision and personal understanding. The collection includes quiz review sheets, assignments, final-exam summaries and personal worked solutions, with typeset formulas and illustrations prepared for study.

**This repository collects personal study materials.** The handwritten EE6309 and EE6316 solutions include selected question excerpts and diagrams for context. The [NTU Library exam-paper portal](https://entuedu.sharepoint.com/sites/lib-repository/exam-question-papers) provides the complete official papers.

**Available now:** six final-exam revision PDFs across EE6302, EE6309 and EE6316, two EE6316 quiz and recap sheets in PDF and editable Markdown, and five handwritten past-exam solution sets. Four sets cover EE6309 from AY2023–2024 through AY2025–2026, and one covers EE6316 AY2025–2026 Semester 1. File names and navigation are in English. Some revision sheets include Chinese memory cues.

## Finding official past examination papers

1. Open the [NTU Library exam-paper portal](https://entuedu.sharepoint.com/sites/lib-repository/exam-question-papers).
2. Sign in with your NTU account.
3. Search the course code, then select the academic year and semester you need.

The Library limits this service to currently enrolled students and staff. See its [access guidance](https://libfaq.ntu.edu.sg/faq/262041). Each course's `past-exams/` folder contains the official link for reference.
